# 支持沟通与隐私 / Support communication and privacy

员工只提交排查所需的最少信息：系统名、时间、错误类型和影响范围。工单和会话只向本人或授权工程师展示。Submit only the system, time, error type and scope needed for support. Conversations and tickets are restricted to their owner or authorised engineers.

聊天与文档导入会先遮盖常见密码、密钥、邮箱和手机号码，但正则规则无法识别所有秘密。请在提交前自行去除敏感内容。Common passwords, keys, email addresses and phone numbers are redacted before storage. Pattern rules cannot detect every secret; remove sensitive content before submitting.

演示环境使用虚构 IT 指引，不能直接作为公司的真实制度。系统只记录模拟通知，除非管理员配置真实 SMTP。Demo guidance is fictional, not a real company policy. Notifications are recorded locally unless an administrator configures SMTP.
