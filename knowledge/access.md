# 账号与权限申请 / Account and access

权限申请须说明所需系统、业务用途、期限和直属主管审批记录。IT 核实后按最小权限原则分配，禁止代用他人账号。Access requests need the system, business purpose, expiry and manager approval. IT grants the minimum access required; never use another person's account.

忘记密码时使用企业身份平台的自助重置入口；身份验证失败则联系 IT。不得在聊天中发送密码、验证码或恢复码。For a forgotten password, use the identity portal's self-service reset. If verification fails, contact IT. Never disclose passwords, one-time codes or recovery codes in chat.

登录失败请记录时间、系统名称和去除个人信息的错误提示。连续失败应暂停重复尝试，确认账号是否锁定。Login troubleshooting: capture the time, system and a redacted error message. Pause repeated attempts and check whether the account is locked.
