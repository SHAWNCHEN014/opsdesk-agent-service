# 服务中断与事故响应 / Outage response

全员无法访问核心业务系统时登记受影响服务、开始时间、影响范围及现有告警。按 P1 创建事故工单并记录升级通知，不等待个人反复尝试。For an outage preventing all employees from accessing a core business service, record the service, onset, scope and alerts. Queue a P1 incident and escalation receipt.

值班工程师先确认影响，联系服务负责人，保留时间线；恢复后验证关键流程并记录解决措施。不要承诺未经验证的恢复时间。The engineer verifies impact, contacts the service owner and keeps a timeline. Verify key workflows after recovery and record the resolution. Never promise an unverified recovery time.

仅咨询事故流程不代表发生事故，不创建工单。单人故障为 P2，知识咨询为 P3；只有真实广泛中断为 P1。Questions about incident procedure are P3 and need no ticket. Individual incidents are P2; actual widespread outages are P1.
