# 设备报修与领用 / Device repair and provision

电脑故障需要设备资产编号、症状和发生时间。不能开机时检查批准的电源和连接；不要拆机或格式化磁盘。Laptop repair requests need the asset identifier, symptoms and time. If it will not turn on, check approved power connections. Do not open the hardware or format the drive.

打印机无法打印时确认设备在线、队列是否暂停、是否只影响本人。取消自己的重复任务后再试一次；不要删除同事的任务。For printing failures, check whether the printer is online, the queue paused and others affected. Cancel only your own duplicate jobs and retry once.

申请电脑或显示器需要岗位用途、交付地点、主管批准和期望日期。IT 完成库存与资产登记后安排领用。Requests for laptops or monitors need the role, location, manager approval and desired date. IT records inventory and assignment before delivery.
